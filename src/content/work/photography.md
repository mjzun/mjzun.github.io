## What I made

From my old portfolio, in my own words:

> Capturing the world around me is my favorite way to practice being present. Being able to be in the moment well enough to not only recognize a beautiful moment, but having the awareness to want to capture it. My love for photography is inherited from my mother. Even within our busy lives, we can always come back to this art form and bond.

This page is the photo series carried over from my old portfolio, where it sat under "My photos", apart from the department design work: nature, architecture, still life, and portraits. A snake-plant close-up, winter trees, a campus rooftop in snow, chess pieces, a portrait in an orange durag.
