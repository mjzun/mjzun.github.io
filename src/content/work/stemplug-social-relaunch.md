## What I made

STEMPLUG makes books, robotics kits, and hands-on STEM programs for underrepresented youth. When I joined as a remote Marketing and Growth Intern in May 2026, its TikTok account had been dormant for roughly three years. I relaunched the account and now produce an ongoing Instagram and TikTok content series that promotes the books, kits, and programs. Alongside the content, I coordinate logistics and promotion for STEMPLUG's STEM workshops and community events.

## Outcome

- Relaunched the TikTok account after roughly three dormant years.
- The Instagram/TikTok series has reached 6,000+ views to date.
- Coordinates logistics and promotion for STEM workshops and community events.

## Process

We started with meetings and audited everything on the old account. I then presented a weekly posting schedule, and the team agreed on it. We automated the posts in HubSpot. There we changed upload times and dates to test what worked. The final schedule ended up very close to the one I first presented.

## Tools

I make the videos and graphics in Canva and CapCut, and I schedule the posts in HubSpot.
