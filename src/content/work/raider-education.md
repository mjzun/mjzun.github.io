## What I made

Raider Education is a student-led leadership and peer-mediation workshop program at Texas Tech. It had ended years earlier, and when it was revived there was no prior material to build on. The assistant director wrote rough content, and I turned it into the program's brand identity and every presentation deck from scratch, plus its flyers, posters, and videos.

The identity shows up on the workshop slides, on the flyers and posters, and in the program brochure. The brochure uses a hexagon layout for the four offerings — workshops for student organizations, trainings for student staff teams, facilitated conversations, and sessions for aspiring student leaders — next to workshop topics like Leadership Begins Here, Conflict with Civility, Keys to Success: Mentorship, and the High Impact Leadership series.

The program's Instagram, @tturaidered, debuted on September 10, 2024. The last upload with a design I made went up on September 17, 2025. Over that year I made about 25 posts.

## The workshop decks

I built about 10 workshop decks, each at least 20 slides, and each went through about four versions. They covered:

- Keys to Success: Mentorship
- Conflict with Civility
- Keeping a Healthy Work-Life Balance
- Leading Through Community Connections
- Together We Lead
- Time Management & Delegation
- Know Your Leadership Style
- Leadership Begins Here
- Charting the Future
- Coalition Building

The decks are Texas Tech property, so they stay on Texas Tech computers.

## Outcome

A revived program with a brand identity and every presentation deck where none had existed, plus its flyers, posters, and videos. This work sat inside the same role where I produced 60+ print and digital pieces for 25–30 events across three units.

## Process

The identity came first. I then workshopped it in the decks, turning the assistant director's rough content into finished slides. I worked from one-on-one meetings with the assistant director and the docs shared with me. After I shipped each iteration, we held periodic check-ins.

## Tools

I did most of the work in Canva, built the decks in PowerPoint and Canva, and edited the videos in CapCut.
