## What I made

Raider Education is a student-led leadership and peer-mediation workshop program at Texas Tech. It had ended years earlier, and it came back with no prior material to reuse. The assistant director wrote rough content for the sessions. I built the program's brand identity and every presentation deck from scratch, plus its flyers, posters, and videos.

I built about 10 workshop decks, each at least 20 slides. Each workshop went through about four versions.

The workshops covered:

- Keys to Success: Mentorship
- Conflict with Civility
- Keeping a Healthy Work-Life Balance
- Leading Through Community Connections
- Together We Lead
- Time Management & Delegation
- Know Your Leadership Style
- Leadership Begins Here
- Charting the Future
- Coalition Building

## Outcome

Every Raider Education presentation deck came out of this work. It sat inside a role where I produced 60+ print and digital pieces for 25–30 events across three units. I kept branding consistent across departments, student organizations, and external partners.

The decks are Texas Tech property and stay on Texas Tech's computers.

## Process

The identity came first. I then workshopped it in the decks, turning the assistant director's rough content into finished slides.

## Tools

I built the decks in PowerPoint and Canva.
