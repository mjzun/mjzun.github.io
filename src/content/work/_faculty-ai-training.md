## What I made

As a Graduate Research Assistant in UNC's Educational Innovation & Technology group (EdIT), I delivered about 10 one-on-one and small-group trainings. They were for School of Education faculty and staff only.

The sessions mainly covered Claude and Lovable. The trainings also covered the everyday technology people teach with: Apple hardware, projectors, and networking.

I did not build a deck or handout for these sessions, so there are no materials to show here.

## Outcome

- About 10 one-on-one and small-group trainings delivered between Aug 2025 and Jul 2026.
- People left with a broader understanding of how to integrate AI into their workflows.
- Faculty returned repeatedly for help ideating agent-based automation of a registration workflow.
- In the same role I resolved 2–3 hardware, AV, and network issues per week across classrooms and offices.

I don't have attendance counts, satisfaction scores, or adoption numbers for these sessions, so I don't claim any.

## Process

A typical session was a one-on-one meeting.

## Automation ideation

Some faculty came back for help ideating agent-based automation of a registration workflow. We held about three ideation sessions, and I taught them Claude Cowork.

The details of the workflow are not cleared for public sharing. I don't know its status after my role ended in July 2026. This was ideation only; I don't claim a built or deployed system.

## Tools

Claude and Lovable were the main subjects of the trainings. Claude Cowork was the tool I taught during the automation ideation.
