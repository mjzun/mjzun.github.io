## What I made

A concept, not a product. While I was a Graduate Research Assistant in UNC's Educational Innovation & Technology group (EdIT), I delivered about 10 one-on-one and small-group trainings for faculty and staff on generative-AI tools. Faculty came back with a further question: could an AI agent automate a registration workflow?

I became the go-to for those follow-up consults, which included ideating agent-based automation for that workflow. This page documents that ideation.
