## What I made

I was selected for the Lovable Ambassador March 2026 cohort, run through the UNC School of Education and Kenan-Flagler Business School. Between March and May 2026 I beta-tested new platform features and shipped five AI-built web apps.

- **LLM Math Standards Evaluation Scorecard** — scores an LLM-generated math lesson against the Common Core math standards. [llmmathsec.lovable.app](https://llmmathsec.lovable.app)
- **DoYourWork** — "Because group work sucks." A group-accountability tool with a student view and a professor view. [doyourwork.lovable.app](https://doyourwork.lovable.app)
- **Wave Weaver** — personalized wave physics problems for middle school, aligned with the NGSS wave-properties standards. [looped584.lovable.app](https://looped584.lovable.app)
- **SchemaScope** — a learning analytics prototype for analogical transfer: students compare two stories and describe how they are alike. [schemascope.lovable.app](https://schemascope.lovable.app)
- **Readscape** — a metacognitive reading lab for upper-elementary students that builds a passage to fit the reader. [reading-lab.lovable.app](https://reading-lab.lovable.app)
