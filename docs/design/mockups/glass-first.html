02About

Matt Zuniga holds an M.A. in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship from UNC Chapel Hill (High Pass in every course) and a B.A. in Digital Media and Professional Communications from Texas Tech.

At Texas Tech he rebuilt a defunct program’s visual identity and produced 60+ pieces for 25–30 events. At EdMotion he built the 10-competitor dashboard and heatmap that changed how the company read its market. Independently, he replicated a published AI-adoption study in Python.

As GPSG Vice President he represented 11,553 graduate and professional students and sat on the Provost’s Generative AI Committee.
