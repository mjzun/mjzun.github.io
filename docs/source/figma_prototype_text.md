### Fashion — "Modeling for local designers."
Lubbock has a very small but tight knit community of designers and creatives of all kinds. The fashion community has provided me a sense of true family in my home away from home and is something I continue to nurture. My close friend Chi and I co-founded a creatives group in Lubbock, with over 100+ members of the community. We talk everyday about projects and opportunities we are apart of to boost productivity.

**First Friday Art Trail** — Downtown Lubbock hosts a monthly event on the first Friday of every month called "First Friday Art Trail". FFAT is a huge gathering of creatives and business owners from all over the city. You can find anything from fashion shows, live music, and metal working at FFAT, showing the best Lubbock has to offer.

**My first show** — May 2024 - Key Johnson

**ZUN, My brand** — ZUN has been in development since 2021. Every creative idea I have is tied to ZUN in some way, through not always fleshed out. It exists for now as mainly a streetwear brand, but over time, it will evolve into a larger brand as a gallery, clothing, and a corner for culture. The name ZUN comes from my last name Zuniga. Which I only used to share with my mother until she got married and changed her last name. I use ZUN on everything as a way to build a "family" amongst the people who interact with my brand. Keeping my legacy alive.

**My Inspiration** — Virgil Abloh is my inspiration in all that I try to create. He applied himself in everything that fascinated him, from architecture, music, and to fashion. Virgil was the head creative director of Louis Vuitton in 2021 when he passed, and he showed me that greatness is the product of pursuing your passions.

**ZUN, Fashion, and the love of art.** — Fashion keeps me grounded and focused on what matters most. Community and innovation. As an art form, fashion is fun to be apart of, but it is a physical reminder to me that I am part of a bigger picture.

### 3D Tech & AI
**Blender** — I use this software mainly for 3D animations of creative projects. The software is simple and user friendly, allowing me to focus on the details and to make something as organic as the shape allows. The best part about Blender is that it can be used to enhance the experience of software applications. 3D modeling and animation allows for the user to form a deeper connection with the website or app.

**Rhino** — I had a short stint in undergrad where I took architecture classes after being inspired by my roommate. He was a second-year student at the time and he worked with me at home to catch me up in Rhino, their main 3D software. Although I did not continue to pursue architecture, I still retain the knowledge and experiment with the software. Rhino truly taught me to trust the process.
Step 1 — Beams and Flooring · Step 2 — Walls and extra structure · Step 3 — Glass

**Prompt Engineering ChatGPT** — This is a quick look at my Prompt Engineering training with ChatGPT. AI is such a useful tool and it can be applied in a healthy way with the right parameters. Here is something I created for Texas Tech University with relation to the Raider Education program I help monitor. I envision this as a two-way practice for RaiderEd to find new presentation participants, as well as helping departments on campus distinguish what programs best align with them.

**Application-based art.** — People seem to have this idea that the STEM fields and art are separate, but that cannot be further from the truth. In fact they are at their best when paired together. That's the point I try to pursue in my technical endeavors.
